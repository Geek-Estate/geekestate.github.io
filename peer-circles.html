GEM Peer Circles

Running a company is lonely. It's not that there's no one to grab lunch with. It's that there's no one who truly understands the decisions in front of you: not employees, life partners, or best friends.

A Peer Circle fixes that.

It's a small, confidential group of fellow PropTech and Built World operators: human beings and professional peers you can trust, who will help you dig deep and level up.

Why you need this

  • Your team can't be your sounding board on everything, and your board and investors have their own agenda.
  • Conferences and panels are broad and polished. Nobody gets into the real numbers.
  • Big networks give you contacts, not people who know your business well enough to tell you the truth.
  • Founders who get honest input month after month make better calls, faster. Most don't have a place to get it.

No fluff, no panels, no performing. Just your actual pain points, tackled head on with others who are doing the work. Just enough structure to hold yourself accountable and track toward your goals. It's where you stop working in the business and start working on the business, and on yourself.

Invest in yourself for 2027.

The experience

  • Experience sharing, not advice. Members share what actually happened to them, not what you should do.
  • Confidentiality. We operate the Circle of Trust. What's shared in the circle stays in the circle unless someone gives explicit permission.
  • Vulnerability. A place where you don't have to perform.
  • Accountability. You'll say what you're going to do, and your circle will ask you about it next month.
  • Personal and professional growth. Your business and you, together.

How it works

Your circle

5 to 8 GEM members, matched based on company stage, ARR, number of employees, and personal situation.

Monthly sessions

One 60-minute Zoom call a month. Calls are never recorded.

In person

Every circle gets a two-night retreat at a time that works for everyone in the group. Your circle picks the location from a shortlist we've already vetted. Included in your Peer Circle fee; you cover your own flights.

Facilitation

Every circle is led by an experienced facilitator who keeps sessions focused, asks the right questions, and holds members to their commitments. Without that structure, peer groups drift into friendly chat instead of the candid, accountable problem-solving that moves businesses forward.

Commitment

One year. Show up every month; missing three sessions means giving up your seat to someone on the waitlist.

January 2027 or June 2027.

Who it's for

Ideal participant: a founder or CEO with product market fit. You're making real decisions about hiring, fundraising, pricing, sales, and product, and you don't have a trusted group of peers outside your company to think those critical decisions through with. You have at least $500k in ARR (and likely more) or $1 million or more in funding. You won't be placed in a circle with someone at a different stage.

Peer circles are matched by stage, revenue, customer, and sector.

You'll get the most out of it if you

  • Want honest feedback, not applause
  • Will show up every month and do the light pre-work
  • Are willing to share real numbers and real problems in confidence
  • Want to help other founders as much as they want help

Not for: anyone looking for deal flow or a sales channel, anyone unable to commit to monthly sessions, or anyone not comfortable with strict confidentiality.

Questions

Who will be in my group?

You will be matched with other founders or CEOs operating a company the same size and stage as yours, some likely with the same ICP. Your competitors will never be placed in your circle.

When will my peer circle start?

January 2027 or June 2027.

A retreat is included. When will I know the exact date and location, and what if I can't make the scheduled date?

We will determine these details within the first month of launching the Peer Circle.

I want all sessions in person. Is there an option for that?

At this time, we feel the richest matches are based on company and founder demographics. Given the focus of GEM and the PropTech community, we don't want to limit ourselves geographically.

How do Peer Circles compare to Hamptons, EO, YPO, Vistage, and others?

GEM Peer Circles are distinctively different for the following reasons:

  • Exclusively PropTech. The perspectives, advice, contacts, and other insights gained in your confidential discussions are hyper-relevant to you and your business. We often hear that founder circles offer more generic perspectives. In GEM, you address your business challenges with implementable solutions from members of the industry who can help open the right doors.
  • Lower floor to enter, lower price point overall.
  • A realistic commitment. About two hours a month: one hour on the call with your peers, plus about 30 minutes before and after to prepare and digest what was discussed. Drop in, then get back to the hustle, which makes it feasible to invest in yourself.
  • GEM retreats are everything. Retreats with GEM help you detach, unmask, and get centered on your personal and professional goals. Add your curated Peer Circle to the mix and you'll experience a level-up you didn't realize was coming.
